## Kit changes it made (reviewed 2026-10-22, kept: 41 tests pass)

- `logo prep`: `--erase` now runs before `--key-bg`, erased corners do not count in the flat-corner test, and an all-transparent logo is not keyed (before, near-black pixels were removed).
- `decal` / `text` / `apply`: a whole-panel colour or pattern no longer floods other panels inside its bounding box (the door lies inside the rear quarter's box). **I measured it on the Thiele draft**: 2.6% of the body pixels change (door and quarter no longer receive each other's fills), nothing else changes, the render looks cleaner.
- `brief`: a layer field `placeholder` ("who allowed it and why") lets `brief show` move on when a stand-in was allowed.
- `cars/rss_gtm_protech_p92_f6/notes.md`: three facts corrected and a dated layout section; `skills/ac-apply`, `skills/ac-logos`: the limits of `--check`/overlap and the `placeholder` field.

## Its process problems and recommendations (open unless marked)

1. `brief show` blocked forever on an allowed placeholder (fixed). 2. `--erase` ran after the `--key-bg` corner check (fixed). 3. Whole-panel fills flooded neighbours (fixed).
4. The overlap guard compares bounding boxes, so almost every fill needs `overlap: true`: use the panel outlines. 5. "Cut off" warnings are false on thin parts (the outline is simplified).
6. Part builds count as rounds: count only full builds. 7. "rear" alone is not a place word, so a missing rear-quarter disc passed; a skipped layer shows as "built".
8. No mirror flip and no greyscale option for logos (tinting the crest or disc gives a flat blob). 9. Patterns cannot avoid logos: a keep-clear mask. 10. `render --each --out x.png` fails with a raw EEXIST error.
11. Sources it found for missing marks (seen in search results only, not opened, nothing downloaded): SC Hassel 1919 at logowik.com and iconape.com; DELS: the series' own site; POTTSPEED, LIFEPLUS, PAPA'S HOUSE, AM: nothing found, ask the team.

## What this run shows

The kit is in working condition for a high-performance model: Opus went through the whole flow, mapped before placing, used the real files, did real fix rounds and finished with a validated export, and the result is the closest to the references of any run except for the art. The remaining gap is **art we do not have** (the plume art, sharper marks, the SC and DELS originals), not the process.
