# Assets used by the Thiele #71 example (not in the repo)

Logos are third-party trademarks; they are never committed (`assets/` is gitignored). This file records where they came from and what is wrong with
each, so they can be re-fetched (with the user's consent, `aclivery logo fetch <url> --name <name> --yes`) or replaced with better ones. The user supplied
these URLs on 2026-10-21 (the first three were Brave image-proxy links; the originals are decoded here).

| File in `assets/logos/` | Source | Size | Problems found |
|---|---|---|---|
| `monster.png` | https://image.pngaaa.com/904/3060904-middle.png | 900x320, alpha flag set but opaque | Wrong variant for this livery: the colour "MONSTER ENERGY ULTRA" wordmark with a white outline; the reference uses a grey/black wordmark with teal ENERGY, and a separate claw. The background is not flat (needs a cut-out). No claw. |
| `porsche.png` | https://images.seeklogo.com/logo-png/31/1/porsche-logo-png_seeklogo-314112.png | 600x600, greyscale, no alpha | Black wordmark on flat white: use `--key-bg`. Small (soft on large panels). |
| `gopro.png` | https://www.pngmart.com/files/22/GoPro-Logo-PNG-HD.png | 2000x944 | White-text version (invisible on a white car): recolour with `--tint`; the blue blocks flatten under `--tint`. Best resolution of the set. |
| `gran-turismo.png` | https://1000marcas.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gran-Turismo-Emblema-500x281.png | 500x281 | Low resolution; the windshield banner needs a white/grey strip and is on another texture. Not used in the example. |
| `motul.png` | https://cdn.freebiesupply.com/logos/large/2x/motul-2-logo-png-transparent.png | 2400x2400 | Good; recolour to dark for a white car. |
| `claw_placeholder.png` | generated by `make_claw.mjs` | 400x500 | A crude three-slash stand-in, **not** the trademark claw. Replace with the real asset (TODO B2/B18). |
